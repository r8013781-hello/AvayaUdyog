## What these are for, and what they are not for

- **For:** the header/banner of a `/locations/{area}` page, once that area's
  evidence gate is satisfied — giving the page a sense of place without
  claiming a project there.
- **Not for:** implying Avaya Udyog completed work in the area. Never caption
  these as "our project in {area}". Never use them in `/projects` or any
  portfolio context.
- **Not a substitute** for the real evidence a location page still needs:
  confirmed coverage and, ideally, a real photographed project in that area.

## Coverage gaps

Only 5 of the 9 priority-tier markets from the territory plan have an image
here (Kolkata generic, New Town, Salt Lake/Sector V, Ballygunge, Howrah).
Alipore, Behala, Mukundapur and North Kolkata do not yet — source on the same
basis (Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA/CC0/CC BY, landscape orientation, no
watermark, verified license via the Commons API) if/when those pages move
toward publishable.
